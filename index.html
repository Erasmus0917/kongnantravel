Company Profile

We provide premium transportation and planning services, and have established partnerships with many local high-end hotels, restaurants, and entertainment venues, offering exclusive deals and special experiences. We also offer private tour guide services. Our guides possess extensive local knowledge and language skills, providing in-depth and personalized commentary. Whether you want to explore Macau's history and culture or discover hidden gems for food and shopping, our guides will meet your needs, making your journey more fulfilling.

Core Business

We have a professional and experienced fleet operations team, dedicated to providing high-quality travel experiences. Our core business is transfer services using the latest Toyota Alphard 40-series 7-seater vehicles, meeting the needs of diverse clients. We provide convenient transfers between airports, hotels, and attractions, ensuring a comfortable journey.

Fleet Maintenance Division

Our own fleet maintenance division, with the parent company Kong Nan Tyre & Auto Products established in 1995, has years of vehicle maintenance experience, providing efficient and reliable vehicle care. We can quickly address vehicle issues, ensuring optimal condition for a superior travel experience.
